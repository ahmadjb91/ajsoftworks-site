Gum Pop! — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 22, 2026

Englishעבריתالعربية

Who we are

Gum Pop! ("the game") is developed by AJ Softworks (Ahmad Jbara), Israel. Contact: ajsoftworks@ajsoftworks.com.

What we collect

We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or any account. The game has no sign-up.

  • Game progress (high score, coins, unlocked flavors, missions, settings) is stored only on your device. We do not receive it.
  • Advertising: the game shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google may collect device information such as the advertising ID (Android Advertising ID / Apple IDFA where you allow it), IP address, device model, operating system, approximate location derived from IP, and ad interaction data. This is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. See also how Google uses data from partner apps.

Your choices

  • iPhone: the game asks for permission to track (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, ads are not personalized using your IDFA.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads / Privacy → Ads).
  • EU / UK / EEA users: a consent form is shown where required by law, and you can choose whether to allow personalized ads.
  • Uninstalling the game deletes all game data stored on your device.

Purchases

If in-app purchases are offered (for example "Remove ads"), payments are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see your payment details.

Children

Gum Pop! is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

Data sharing & security

We do not sell personal information. Apart from the advertising and store providers named above, we do not share data with third parties. Data sent to Google is transmitted using encryption.

Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version will always be on this page with its effective date.
